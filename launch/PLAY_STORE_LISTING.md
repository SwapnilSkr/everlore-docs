### Notes on what is deliberately absent

- **No mention of mature or NSFW content.** It is real, it is opt-in, and it is
  declared truthfully in the Content rating questionnaire — but a store listing
  that advertises it invites the exact policy review that removes AI roleplay
  apps. Declare it; do not market it.
- **No competitor names.** Play rejects listings that reference other apps.
- **No "unlimited", "best", or "#1"** — unsubstantiated superlatives are a
  listing-rejection reason.
- Prices are never stated in the description; Play Console is the only price
  source.
